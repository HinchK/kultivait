# Energy estimation: local-compute Wh only, from a measured versioned coefficient table, labeled-estimate everywhere

The ledger gains a third currency beside ADR 0005's notional/metered duality: every local dispatch record carries `est_wh` — estimated local-compute energy computed as `(tokens_in × prefill + tokens_out × decode)` against a committed, versioned coefficient table (`energy-v1-20260909`: Wh per 1K tokens for five parameter classes 1.5b–14b, measured sudo-free on the dev machine's M4 Pro via zeus-apple-silicon IOReport energy windows, N=5 medians, idle-subtracted, provenance and raw samples in `experiments/energy_measurement/`). The table is embedded as constants in `src/kultivait/energy.py` (installed tools have no experiments tree), tagged with its version on every ledger record (`energy_model`), and overridable by config — values, never provenance. Cloud and CLI dispatches record `0.0`: their energy is not ours to claim. Counterfactual avoided-Wh — datacenter energy for tokens never sent — is rejected outright: it requires PUE and per-model server-energy figures we cannot ground, and the project's own history (#139 removed fabricated "31 Wh / 0.9 kWh" landing claims) sets the bar this design answers with codified invariants: every display says *est.* and names the table version, values round to 2 significant figures, no live-growing counters ever, and `est_wh` is zero on non-local rows — all test-enforced. `latency_s` lands in the ledger now as forward-looking substrate (duration-based refinement is impossible retroactively). Grounded in the energy research register (`docs/research/2026-09-09-local-inference-energy.md`, 19 sourced figures) and the measured table (#168; 9/10 sanity gates passed, the 14B decode cell honestly flagged 11% above the extrapolated literature bound with the disposition pre-declared).

## Considered Options

- **Datacenter counterfactual (avoided-Wh)** — rejected: ungroundable claims (PUE, server energy per model unknown for our mix); exactly the class of number the project fabricated-and-removed in #139.
- **Live power measurement at dispatch time** — rejected: every credible macOS instrument is either sudo-bound (`powermetrics`, reproduced locally) or a background sampler daemon; both violate the zero-root posture and add a runtime dependency for marginal precision.
- **Literature-only coefficients** — rejected as primary: we have the sudo-free instrument and the hardware; self-measured figures with committed provenance beat adapted server-class numbers (GreenBench's own "package" figure was shown by the register to be compute-domain-only, not wall power).
- **Duration × power estimation** — rejected for v1: the ledger records no durations today (nothing to integrate retroactively); token-based coefficients work now; `latency_s` is recorded so a duration-based refinement can be evaluated against real data later.
- **Per-model coefficients (every distinct model measured)** — rejected: maintenance treadmill; five parameter classes capture the bandwidth-bound energy trend (measured medians monotone 0.038→0.388 Wh/1K across 1.5b→14b) at the precision an estimate deserves.
- **Wall-power coefficients (system envelope)** — rejected for v1: the sudoless instrument reads IOReport domains (CPU+GPU+GPU-SRAM+DRAM), a compute+DRAM estimate below wall power; claiming wall would require the sudo cross-check. Displayed as *est.* with the domain-sum caveat in the provenance README.
- **Rendering Wh in the landing hero** — rejected: the landing is out of scope for this effort (deploy separate) and post-#139 energy copy on marketing surfaces deserves its own deliberate pass with the real numbers in hand.
- **Recomputing estimates at read time (harvest-side)** — rejected: coefficients version at record time (`energy_model` tag per row); read-time recomputation would silently rewrite history when the table updates. Old rows keep their table's numbers; new rows carry the new tag.

## Consequences

- `ledger.record()` gains `est_wh` (0.0 non-local), `energy_model` (always the active table version), and `latency_s` (all dispatches); harvest sums filter on the local flag.
- `kultivait energy`-related display surfaces (harvest block, dashboard panel) must satisfy the five invariants, enforced by tests in the implementation ticket.
- The coefficient table updates by measurement re-runs committed to `experiments/energy_measurement/` with a new version tag — never by editing values in place.
- Unknown model names bucket to `default_class` ("8b") unless overridden; the class-resolution rule (parameter-count parse, nearest bucket) is deterministic and tested.
- The 14B decode cell's flagged bound rides to the sanity eval (#171), which pre-registers its own final acceptance bars and may re-measure that cell on a quiet machine.
