# Corpus & label assembly: serving-shape pairs, tiered truth, teacher rewrites, synthetic-led with real anchors

Training pairs are **serving-shape mlx-lm chat JSONL**: system = the live preprocessor contract instructions, user = the last user message (tool-blind per the landed contract — schemas never enter), assistant = the complete contract JSON (`analysis`, `rewrite`, `judge`) — the model trains on exactly what serving executes, zero train/serve skew. Ground truth is **tiered by the trustworthiness of the signal that produced it**, never copied from the incumbent judge (that would clone the calibration weakness #14 measured): **gold** = human toll picks and tollbooth overrides (a present human judged local-vs-frontier with real money on the line); **silver** = expired counterfactuals, auto-policy outcomes, and frontier-dispatch success/failure signals (biased by the policy that produced them, weighted down); **bronze** = capability-eval accuracy outcomes (indirect — which target could actually serve); **unlabeled pool** = escalations and ledger prompts, whose "cloud-worthy" rule is a routing heuristic, not a verdict label. Fit values are **regressed from verdict-tier labels** (gold-local → fits in the < 0.65 band, gold-frontier → ≥ 0.85, with spread the corpus ticket's synthesis owns), not imitated. Rewrite labels are **teacher-written per pair**: a frontier teacher writes the rewrite for every corpus pair, given the original prompt and the verdict tier as context — a local-bound rewrite keeps the original's economy, a frontier-bound rewrite is made self-contained (decision #7's per-target split, emitted for the verdict-consistent target). ADR 0009's cross-family rule extends to **judge labels** (the teacher that fits targets must differ from any family with a stake in the verdict); the **rewriter teacher carries no cross-family constraint** — it is generative, not judgmental, and one provider may teach rewrites while another teaches fits. Volume is **synthetic-led with real anchors**: every real harvested prompt (escalations, toll/preprocessed cases, ledger prompts) forms the anchor set — seeding synthetic generation in the gates.py escalation-distillation pattern extended to corpus generation — while **every real verdict-bearing case is permanently held out for eval and never trained on** (too precious to burn; ~24 today, growing with the flywheel). Synthetic pairs scale to a v1 target of **~1–2k** (~35 min/epoch at 1k on the M4 Pro per the fine-tuning landscape). The first training run gates on: the full real held-out eval set exists AND ≥ 500 synthetic pairs pass dedup/quality bars. The flywheel: each future harvest adds real anchors and grows the held-out set; synthetic regenerates around them.

## Considered Options

- **Simplified instructional pair format**: rejected — trains a different task than the one served; the serving prompt is the contract.
- **Equal-weight voting across sources**: rejected — a heuristic store and a deliberate human choice carry very different error rates; tiers exist because trust differs.
- **Frontier dispatch outcomes as the only truth**: rejected — survival bias (frontier mostly ran where policy sent it) and it discards the strongest signal (human picks).
- **Incumbent rewrites as labels**: rejected — the ceiling becomes the 4B model being replaced.
- **Human-authored rewrite seeds amplified by a teacher**: rejected for v1 — human-in-the-loop cost makes corpus scaling manual; the per-pair teacher covers v1, human seeds may join later.
- **Real-only corpus**: rejected — ~24 verdict-bearing entries cannot fine-tune anything; the flywheel never starts.
- **10k+ synthetic corpus**: rejected for v1 — ~5.5h/epoch and a quality-review project of its own; ~1–2k fits the probe-and-iterate cadence.

## Consequences

- The corpus builder reads three sources (ledger, escalation store, capability-eval artifacts) and emits two disjoint artifacts: the training JSONL (synthetic pairs anchored on real prompts) and the permanent held-out eval set (real verdict-bearing cases, gold-labeled first).
- Teacher usage splits cleanly by label kind: fits/confidence from a judge teacher under the cross-family rule; rewrites from a rewriter teacher with no constraint — the teacher ticket inherits both roles and their provider choices.
- Fit-value synthesis (band placement + spread within band) is owned by the corpus generation step, guided by the tier of each label.
- Ledger fields gained nothing new; no schema change — the corpus builder is a read-side consumer of the harvest.
- Term canonized in CONTEXT.md: **Anchor set**.
