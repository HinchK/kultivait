FINDINGS (verified this phase):
- The search service is OpenSearch 2.11, single node, deployed via the
  opensearch-operator chart at deploy/search/values.yaml.
- Index templates live in infra/search/templates/*.json; there are 14 of them,

- Ingestion goes through the queue worker src/workers/ingest.py at a measured

- The largest index, logs-2026-08, is 1.9 TB with 12 shards; shard 7 runs hot

  effort and is caused by the routing round-robin added in 5.1.

DECISIONS (made this phase, with reasons):

  compatibility plugin this repo's dashboards depend on. Revisit after the
  dashboards migration closes.
- Rolling restart, not blue-green: the single-node topology makes blue-green
  require a second 2 TB volume that FinOps declined (cost center CAP-118).
- Snapshot before every node-level step: the existing snapshot repo
  (s3://corp-search-snapshots, 30-day retention) is sufficient; do NOT create
  a new bucket.

CONSTRAINTS (must hold in the plan):
- The upgrade window is the Saturday 02:00-06:00 UTC slot; search may be read-
  only for at most 45 minutes or the SLA breach pages the on-call VP.
- The ingest worker cannot be paused longer than 30 minutes without RabbitMQ
  hitting its 4 GB memory alarm — the queue currently idles at 1.2 GB.
- Any plan touching shard rebalancing must quote the 5.1 routing change, since
  reverting it is a customer-visible behavior change requiring a changelog entry.
