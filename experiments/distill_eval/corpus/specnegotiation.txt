SPEC NEGOTIATION — designing export limits for the API

PM: Enterprise buyers keep asking for bulk export. Legal needs an audit trail.
What's the smallest thing we can ship in 6.2?

Eng: The naive version is a button that streams a CSV. CONSTRAINT: our largest
tenant has 2.1 million rows in projects alone; streaming that through the web
tier times out the 60-second gateway limit at around 400k rows.

PM: So cap the first version?

Eng: Cap it, but at what? Three options on the table. One: hard cap at 50k rows
with a "filter to narrow" message — shippable in a week, useless for the buyers
who asked. Two: async job writing to S3 with a signed URL, expiry 24 hours —
two weeks, needs the job queue. Three: paginate a sync endpoint — meets nobody's
definition of bulk.

PM: Legal's audit requirement applies to any export: who, what query, when, and
row count. That has to be in the existing audit_events table, not a new store —
compliance already built their dashboards on audit_events.

Eng: Noted. DECISION then: option two, async to S3, and every job writes an
audit_events row with action="export.completed", the query hash, row count, and
the requester's user id. The signed URL expiry of 24 hours matches the security
review's data-retention guidance from last quarter (doc sec-review-2026-q2).

PM: What are the job's own limits?

Eng: Proposal: max 5 million rows per job, max 2 concurrent jobs per tenant,
and a global cap of 20 concurrent so the workers stay under the 70% CPU
ceiling ops enforces. Formats: CSV first, Parquet in 6.3 — Parquet needs a
column-typing pass we don't have yet.

PM: Enterprise asked for "everything", meaning cross-workspace.

Eng: Cross-workspace export is explicitly out for 6.2 — it drags in the
workspace-permission model and legal wants per-workspace audit scoping first.
Record it as deferred with that reason, or it will resurface as a bug.

PM: Agreed, deferred. Pricing tier?

Eng: Ops' cost model says a 5M-row job costs us about $0.60 in S3 PUT + worker
time. Finance wants exports gated to the Scale tier and up. DECISION: feature-
flag EXPORT_JOBS with tier gating at the flag check, not in the controller —
the flag service already knows tiers.

PM: Timeline sanity check?

Eng: Two weeks to the 6.2 feature freeze on the 15th, tight but real, PROVIDED
the audit_events schema change lands this week — it's additive (two nullable
columns, query_hash and row_count), migration number 20260915091447. Anything
later slips us to 6.3 with Parquet.
